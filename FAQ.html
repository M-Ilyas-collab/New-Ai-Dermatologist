Is my data safe? expand_more

Data security is our top priority. All images and personal health information (PHI) are encrypted both in transit and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We are fully HIPAA compliant and never share your data with third-party advertisers or insurance companies.

What conditions can it detect? expand_more

Our library currently supports the identification of over 50+ unique skin conditions, categorized into:

  • check_circle Melanocytic lesions (Moles)
  • check_circle Basal Cell Carcinoma
  • check_circle Psoriasis & Eczema
  • check_circle Acne & Viral infections
Can I use this for my children? expand_more

Yes, DermaAI is safe for all ages. However, pediatric skin can present differently than adult skin. We recommend that a parent or legal guardian manages the account and captures the images for anyone under the age of 18.

How do I get the best results? expand_more

For the highest accuracy, please ensure:

  1. The area is well-lit (natural daylight is best).
  2. The camera is held 10-15cm away from the skin.
  3. The image is sharp and in focus.
  4. The skin is clean and free of makeup or creams.
